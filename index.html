I am currently an M.Sc. student in mathematics at Simon Fraser University, where I am fortunate to be supervised by Professor Matt DeVos. Before this, I received a B.E. from Beijing Jiaotong University and an M.S. from Texas A&M University, both in computer science.

I am interested in algebraic and discrete structures in general. In particular, I am fascinated by understanding how algebraic language can be applied to interpret phenomena in combinatorics. I will be applying to Ph.D. positions this fall, specializing in combinatorial algebraic geometry and algebraic combinatorics.

Things I am (and/or will be) eagerly learning: commutative algebra, algebraic geometry, algebraic topology, geometry of matroids, tropical geometry, etc.
